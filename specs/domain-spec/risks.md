> Risks with Impact=HIGH and quantifiable mitigation are flagged as NFR candidates.

| R-001 | **Anthropic releases comprehensive official CLI/TUI tool.** Anthropic owns the protocol and has distribution advantage (SDK + Claude ecosystem). A full official tool would significantly erode Forge MCP's adoption. | business | Medium | HIGH | open | Focus on capabilities Anthropic is unlikely to prioritize: runtime security auditing, enterprise compliance reporting. | R-004 | **Security auditing false positives erode trust.** Runtime behavioral analysis is inherently heuristic. Virtual scrolling for large datasets. Profile ratatui rendering hot paths. Consider differential rendering (only update changed cells). NFR candidate: yes (60fps at 100 events/sec, < 5MB RSS). Security focus: no. | CAP-006, CAP-008, ASM-004 |
